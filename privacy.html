Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 May 2026

Short version: We collect your email address only if you give it to us voluntarily. We use it to send you what you asked for. We never sell or share your email list. You can unsubscribe at any time and we will delete your address promptly.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by SafeAI, a sole trader based in Cork, Ireland. We provide AI awareness training, compliance guidance, and digital resources for organisations navigating the EU AI Act.

Data controller: Marcela, SafeAI
Contact: marcela@safeai.ie

2. What data we collect and why

Contact form submissions

When you use our contact or booking form, we collect your name and email address. This data is processed through Formspree and is used solely to respond to your enquiry. Formspree's privacy policy applies to their handling of this data.

Newsletter and email list

If you subscribe to our mailing list, we collect your email address. We use this to send you updates, articles, and information about SafeAI services. The legal basis for this processing is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Data retention: We keep your email address only for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, your address is deleted promptly. We never keep email addresses longer than necessary, and we never sell or share our mailing list with third parties — ever.

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by emailing marcela@safeai.ie and asking us to remove you.

Digital product purchases

If you purchase a digital product (guide, checklist, or resource) through our website, the transaction is processed by Gumroad. SafeAI does not collect or store your payment card details. Gumroad acts as the merchant of record and their privacy policy governs payment data. We may receive your name and email address from Gumroad to fulfil your order and provide post-purchase support.

AI tools used in our work

SafeAI uses AI writing and productivity tools internally to prepare course materials, draft articles, and support our work. We do not input client personal data into any third-party AI tool. Any data processed this way is anonymised or fictional before use.

Website cookies

This website uses essential cookies only — small files stored on your device that are strictly necessary for the site to function (for example, remembering that you have dismissed the cookie banner). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or analytics that identify individual users.

Because we use only essential cookies, we do not require your consent to set them under the ePrivacy Regulations. However, you can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings.

3. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We use the following processors to operate our services:

  • Formspree — contact form processing
  • Gumroad — digital product payments and delivery

Email addresses collected via our newsletter signup are stored securely by SafeAI and are not shared with any third party.

Each processor operates under their own privacy policy and, where required, a data processing agreement with us.

4. Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erase your data (the right to be forgotten)
  • Restrict how we process your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal)
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, email marcela@safeai.ie. We will respond within one month.

5. How to complain

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Irish supervisory authority:

Data Protection Commission (DPC)
21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28
www.dataprotection.ie

We would appreciate the opportunity to address your concern directly before you contact the DPC — please email us first.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure. Our website is served over HTTPS. Access to any data we hold is restricted to SafeAI staff.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect when it was last revised. Material changes will be communicated by email to subscribers.

Questions? Email marcela@safeai.ie — we are happy to help.
